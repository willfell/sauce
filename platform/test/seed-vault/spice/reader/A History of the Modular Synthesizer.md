> A patch cable is a design decision made audible.

[//]: # (READER_CONTENT)

The modular synthesizer never fully died; it went dormant and returned as Eurorack, a shared format that let small makers interoperate.

What the format standardized was not sound but connection: a common voltage range and a common jack made a thousand tiny companies compatible.
