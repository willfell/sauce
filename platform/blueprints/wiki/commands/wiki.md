# /wiki — Wiki Navigation

Open the wiki hub, create sections and pages, or find content by title or tag.

## Navigate

Open the wiki hub: the hub is at `spice/wiki/Wiki.md`. Use the nav button or open it directly.

## Create

- **New section:** Click `+ New Section` on any hub or section note. Enter a name; a new section hub is created in a sub-folder.
- **New page:** Click `+ New Page` on any hub or section note. Enter a title; the page lands in the current section folder.

## Find a page

Search by title or tag using the DocSearch strip on any hub or section view.

## Move a page

Open the page and click **Move** in the leaf actions bar. Pick the destination section.
