### 4. Monthly cohesion dashboard

The user wants to see, for any given month (e.g. July 2026):

- Total spending (budget actuals for that month)
- Total debt paydown (sum of debt-linked paycheck expenses with `paid: true`)
- Debt balance delta MoM
- Headline math: **income − spending − debt paydown = net cashflow** (red if negative; green if positive)

Design decision needed: new `Monthly-<YYYY-MM>.md` note? Or extend `Budget-<YYYY-MM>.md` to also be the monthly hub? Or new `spice/finance/months/` sub-area? Resolve at brainstorm.
