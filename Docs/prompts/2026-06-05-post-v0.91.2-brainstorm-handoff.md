## Why this handoff has this shape

The previous handoff pattern (post-v0.89.1) was to read cycle-status + the most recent result-doc + jump into brainstorm. That pattern broke this session because cycle-status was STALE (still claimed v0.89.0 current; HEAD was actually v0.91.2). The audit doc is the load-bearing fix — it captures the state synthesis the brainstorm needs, regardless of what cycle-status says.

**The 4 brainstorm shapes are deliberately scoped to be the audit-evidence-driven candidates**, not exhaustive. Open candidates not in the 4 shapes — distill-week (deferred-thrice), slice-E brain-map rollups, FLN-v89-7 `--subscribe` flag, FLN-v89-8 E.164 normalization, FLN-v89-9 microscope-wikilink contract, FLN-v89-10 toast diagnostic, FLN-v891-1 manifest substitution-token validator, FLN-v891-2 write-run-note Step 2a cleanup — all stay queued in `cycle-status.md` and the audit doc references them. They're available but not the headlining candidates because the vault evidence doesn't immediately demand them.

**Layer 6 (Shape 1) is the recommended primary candidate** because (a) FLN-v89-P0-1's worsening trend on accuris is the highest-cost open finding (it's actively producing wrong output every morning), (b) it composes naturally onto the v0.91.x layer-cake pattern that's been the explicit work product of the last 4 cycles, (c) the body-composition gap is the only major contract-failure class without a deterministic backstop. But it's open to debate — Shape 2 (v0.92.0 required-plugins) has the advantage of being plan-ready and closing a real headspace-side gap with one short cycle. Bundling Shape 2 + Shape 1 sequentially is a coherent ~2-cycle path.
