## What shipped

Guardrail #2 — a supersession-depth ceiling that halts the accreting-1:1 runaway
(one lineage spun 27 supersessions and merged nothing) that guardrail #1's
breadth cap could not see. Root cause: a convergence-free adversarial treadmill
on a program-sized slice, with neither supersession-creation surface checking
lineage depth.

  the second-refutation step probes the query and escalates to the Director at

  (absolute) `source_board`/`kanban_board` — `card-intake.js` `renderEpicAtlas`
