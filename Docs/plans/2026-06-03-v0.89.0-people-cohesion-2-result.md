## Why

**Original framing (verbatim):** "incorporating people more prompting wise within the claude cowork mechanisms that we have going on with scheduled jobs" — user, /remote-control session 2026-06-03 (`people-cohesion-2`).

The arc started in v0.88.0 (slice A — single canonical person creation path, relaxed naming pattern, suggester-loop attendee autocomplete) made `+ New Person` clean but cowork's atomic-note output still mentioned people as plain `**Name**` strings. Person notes accumulated zero backlinks from cowork activity even though cowork is the workshop's primary signal source about who-was-doing-what-when.

This cycle closes that gap. Every gather-skill output that mentions humans now flows through `cowork:resolve-person` and emits `[[Person]]` wikilinks on hit. The voice rule extends the convention to LLM free-form composition. Forward from this deploy, person notes accumulate backlinks at every morning briefing / midday tripwire / EOD review / weekly + monthly review / synthesis tick. Brain-map rollups (slice E) become signal-rich.
